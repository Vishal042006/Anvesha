# ANVESHA-DATA-v1.0 DATASET CARD

## 1. OVERVIEW
- **Publisher:** National Crime Records Bureau (NCRB), Ministry of Home Affairs, Government of India.
- **Coverage:** 35 States/UTs, 594 Canonical Districts, 188 Reference Cities.
- **Period:** 2001 to 2023 (23 calendar years).

## 2. COMPOSITION & PROVENANCE
- **Track A (Official Ground Truth):** 10,678 records (2001–2014 NCRB Gazetteers). Zero synthetic rows.
- **Track B (Synthetic Extended Analysis):** 17,761 total rows (10,678 official + 7,083 synthetic gapfill rows 2015–2023).
- **Track C (City Statistical Layer):** 1,107 official 2023 city observations across 5 metric domains.
